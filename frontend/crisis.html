Get Immediate Help

TARN is not a crisis service, and entries are not monitored. If you are experiencing emotional distress or having thoughts of suicide, call or text 988 to connect with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Outside the United States

The numbers above work in the United States only. TARN does not yet carry verified crisis resources for other countries, and showing you a number that will not connect would be worse than saying so.

findahelpline.com lists verified crisis lines by country.

Nothing you write in TARN is read by a person, reviewed by a healthcare professional, or monitored for signs of distress. It cannot detect an emergency and it cannot contact anyone on your behalf.
